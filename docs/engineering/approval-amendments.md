# Product direction and plan amendments

Current authenticated product-owner direction changes repository-authored plans and requirements within higher-priority platform and organizational controls.

## Delegated mode

Record the change in the active **work.md**, update affected acceptance criteria and technical plan, reassess consequence, and continue. Technical details, expected files, and implementation sequence may change without a new product decision.

Ask for a decision when the change introduces a new recurring cost, material data exposure, irreversible external effect, production action outside an existing authorization, public commitment, or a conflict with an explicit product constraint.

## Formal mode

Formal projects retain the model-v2 amendment process. New intent, decision, plan, and authorization revisions preserve the evidence chain; use **docs/engineering/feature-planning.md**.

## Evidence rule

A work record can record the source of a decision but cannot prove a human approval. Do not infer approval from an editable checkbox, PR authorship, or an agent-authored comment.
